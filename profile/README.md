## What we do

Predictive models are deployed among people who have their own goals — applicants, sellers, users, agents. When they adapt to a model, the model changes the world it learned from.

We build the theory and tools for learning in these settings:

- **Strategic classification** — classifiers that account for strategic behavior
- **Performative prediction** — predictions that shape the outcomes they forecast
- **Welfare-centric ML** — learning objectives that reflect the welfare of the people models serve
- **Aligned language models** — LMs whose incentives align with their users
